Read with care

  • The recordings are not independent: one rider, one phone, overlapping roads. A spot hit on several rides is several measurements of one spot. Pooled rates are descriptive, not a sample estimate.
  • All three rides were on Voi Voiager 5 scooters (confirmed by Voi customer support, personal communications, 3 October 2026 for ride 1 and 8 October 2026 for rides 2 and 3), so pooling does not mix models. Individual scooters may still differ (tyre wear and pressure, fork condition): each ride was on a different scooter (SZ22, UPAR, BTLZ).
  • Recording 2's impacts 25–37 form a regular series (~0.6–1.2 s apart at ~8 km/h); not checked. They make up most of the largest pooled hotspot, which recording 3 also hit (its impacts 1–5), so a rough surface there is plausible, but not confirmed.
  • Distance comes from GPS-reported speed, which under-read walking speed in the validity check (report).
  • Hotspots here are clustered on the pooled impacts, so their numbers differ from the per-recording reports.
