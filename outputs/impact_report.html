Limitations

  • One ride, one rider, one phone mount. Counts will vary with route, speed, tyre pressure and mount stiffness.
  • The 1.0 g threshold, severity bands and 1.5× wobble cut-off are analyst choices for this study, not established injury or comfort criteria.
  • Impacts are positioned from 1 Hz GPS (typical accuracy ~10 m); road assignment near junctions can be wrong.
  • Kerbs, drop-kerbs, speed humps and manhole covers register as impacts too; not every impact is a pothole.
